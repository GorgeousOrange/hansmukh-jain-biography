01 / Profile

A life and body
of work

According to the biographical profile supplied for this website, Hansmukh Jain was born on 26 November 1961 in Khunta (Shrinagar), in the Dhariyawad area of Rajasthan. The profile lists his residence as Nandanvan, Himvan, Dhariyawad. His stated areas of study include Jain geography, worship and consecration rites, temple and residential Vastu, astrology, Samudrika Samhita and spiritual studies.

The supplied profile also describes involvement in religious and cultural activities, temple construction, consecration ceremonies, educational institutions and community service. This website organises the available information and provides a place to add supporting records and independent publications.
